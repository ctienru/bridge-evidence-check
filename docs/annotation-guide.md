# Annotation guide

Status: 77 of 100 clauses reviewed by Terri. A clause counts only when its `Reviewed:` marker is `yes`. Revision 01 applied on 2026-11-09 (issue #15). The clauses changed and the frozen gold items examined are listed in [`annotation-guide-revision-01.md`](./annotation-guide-revision-01.md).

Guideline version: 1. Machine-level names (condition IDs, status codes, enums, gold format) are defined in [`contracts.md`](./contracts.md); when this guide and the contract disagree, the contract wins and this guide is corrected.

This guide is followed by everyone who writes gold: the human annotator and the candidate-proposing model (a non-Claude model). The extractor (Claude) does not read it and its output never enters gold.

Clause IDs: `P` principles, `S` statuses, `A` assertion, `T` timepoints, `C` per-condition rules, `K` claims, `G` clinical judgement, `W` keyword scan and cue words, `B` blind draft, `L` adjudication log, `U` uncertainty reasons, `R` reliability checks and guide revision. Every clause ends with a `Reviewed:` marker. Clinical judgement clauses (`G-xx`) also carry a `Source:` line; gold facts decided by one of them record its ID in `guideline_clause`.

All examples in this guide are synthetic sentences written for the guide. None is quoted from MTSamples, from a published case report, or from any case document.

## 1. Scope and principles

- **P-01** Label only what the system can see: the text of the files under `cases/<variant_id>/docs/` plus `input.yaml`. Nothing in `case.yaml`, in the case story, or in the annotator's head produces a gold fact. If a fact is needed for a definite gold (for example "this measurement is the initiation baseline"), it must be written into a visible document marked `doc_origin: authored`. Example: the case story says the consult took place on the initiation day, but no document says so → the BMI fact stays `unknown` until an authored referral letter states the date. `Reviewed: yes`
- **P-02** Extractor output never becomes gold. The candidate-proposing model is a different vendor's model, called through the API and recorded at snapshot-level model ID; its candidates are suggestions to verify, not gold. The same model drafts authored documents (route 1). Its snapshot-level model ID is recorded in `case.yaml` as `documents[].drafted_by` for every `doc_origin: authored` document, and in `adjudication.jsonl` (`drafted_by`, §10) for every candidate; a rewrite in the dirty ladder records the rewriting model the same way in that level's `case.yaml`. Example: the proposing model lists an A1c the annotator cannot find in the text → no fact is written; the extractor's output is likewise never consulted. `Reviewed: yes`
- **P-03** One human makes the final judgement on every scored gold fact and every condition status (`review: human_verified`). There is no "two models agree" shortcut and no sampling-based sign-off. Example: the proposing model and the keyword scan both point at the same A1c sentence; the fact is still opened and judged by the annotator before it enters gold. `Reviewed: yes`
- **P-04** Gold is produced by two routes (contract §8, §9). Route 1, authored documents: a spec (`case.yaml` fact list) is written first; a non-Claude model drafts the documents and returns a fact_id → verbatim quote map; deterministic checks confirm every quote exists verbatim, the value is inside the quote, the assertion cue is in the quote's sentence, and the requested dirty wording appears; Terri reads the final documents and checks both directions (every spec fact is written; no clinical fact beyond the spec). Route 2, real text: Terri blind-labels statuses and inventories facts first (§9), one non-Claude model proposes candidates with quote locations, a deterministic keyword scan (§8) lists uncovered hits, and Terri verifies every scored fact one by one. Example: in a case with an MTSamples consult note, an authored referral letter and an authored PA draft, the consult note is labelled by route 2 and the two authored documents by route 1. `Reviewed: yes`
- **P-05** Gold is frozen before the pipeline first touches the case (rolling freeze). Later corrections bump `gold_version` and are written to `changes` with date and reason; gold is never silently changed to match model output. Example: a wrong `event_date` found after the first run → `gold_version` 1 → 2, a `changes` entry with the date, the clause and the reason; the old value is not overwritten in place. `Reviewed: yes`
- **P-06** A fact is labelled where it is written. The same fact repeated in two documents is two gold facts (one per `doc_id`); the same fact repeated in one document is one gold fact unless the repetitions describe different events. Record a separately documented administration or regimen change, such as a bolus, dose change, start, or stop, as a separate `medications` fact. A restatement of the same event remains one fact even if it adds the route or vehicle. Two facts of different fields or value keys may share one span (an ID and a DOB in one sentence). Example: "BMI 44.3" in the clinic note and "BMI 44.3 at the start of semaglutide" in the referral letter are two facts, one per `doc_id`; "BMI 44.3" written twice in the same note's vitals and assessment is one fact. `Reviewed: yes`
- **P-07** Gold for MTSamples documents stores `start`, `end` and `quote_sha256` only, never the quote text. The same rule applies to `blind_draft.json` and `adjudication.jsonl`: refer to text by `doc_id`, offsets and hash, not by copying it. Example: a fact in an MTSamples document is `{"doc_id": "D2-clinic-note", "start": 812, "end": 833, "quote_sha256": "…"}` and the quote appears nowhere in the repository. `Reviewed: yes`
- **P-08** Condition statuses in gold are decided by the annotator from the facts, by hand, without running the production rule engine. A disagreement between the blind status and the status implied by the facts is investigated and its cause recorded (§10). Example: blind status `bmi_path_b.htn` `not_supported`; the facts give `insufficient` (one reading of 140/88) → the §10 line records the cause "boundary reading mistaken for counter-evidence". `Reviewed: yes`
- **P-09** Expected absence (`expected_absent[]`) is asserted only after both the inventory (§9) and the keyword scan (§8) have been checked for that field; "the model did not list it" is never enough. Example: `{"field": "lifestyle", "kind": "activity"}` is written to `expected_absent` only after the inventory row for activity says `absent` and the scan shows no uncovered hit for exercise, walking or gym. `Reviewed: yes`
- **P-10** Clinical judgement clauses (§7) exist so that such judgements are explicit, sourced and countable, not so that they are presumed correct. The report states how many facts were decided by them. Example: the report states "14 facts decided by G-xx clauses". `Reviewed: yes`

## 2. Statuses

The five codes are defined in contract §1. This section says when an annotator picks each one. One synthetic example each; the status is for the named condition only.

- **S-01** `supported`: at least one verifiable quote from the right patient, with assertion present / patient and the required timepoint, establishes the condition. Example: "Weight 128 kg, height 170 cm, BMI 44.3 at the start of semaglutide on 2025-03-15." → `bmi_path_a` `supported`. `Reviewed: yes`
- **S-02** `not_supported`: positive counter-evidence or evidence of absence. Negation of the concept, an explicitly complete list that lacks the required item, or a BMI below the threshold. A lab or a reading below a threshold is not counter-evidence: an A1c below the prediabetes range (contract §3.2 step 6) and a BP reading with systolic ≤ 140 and diastolic ≤ 90 (`htn_not_supported_by_readings: false`) leave the sub-result `insufficient`. Example: "Denies diabetes or prediabetes; A1c 5.2% last month." → `bmi_path_c.prediabetes` `not_supported`. `Reviewed: yes`
- **S-08** A denial of an organ system or a disease class (denies kidney disease, liver disease, cardiac disease, lung disease) is not a negated mention of a tracked concept (`ckd`, `mash`, `mi`, `hfpef`, `osa`); it produces no fact and leaves the sub-result `insufficient` when nothing else is written. Only a denial that names the concept or one of its listed wordings (C-xx) is labelled `negated`. `Reviewed: no`
- **S-03** `insufficient`: nothing is said, or what is said cannot carry a conclusion either way (wrong timepoint, no diagnosis behind a lab, only advice given, payer unknown). Example: "Patient was encouraged to eat more vegetables." → `lifestyle.nutrition` `insufficient`. `Reviewed: yes`
- **S-04** `conflict`: two verified values for the same fact (contract §4) sit on opposite sides of the threshold the condition uses, or a diagnosis is both asserted and negated at the same timepoint. Example: a clinic note "BMI 29.6 at initiation" and the referral letter "BMI 30.4 when Wegovy was started" → `bmi_path_b.bmi` `conflict`; `bmi_path_c.bmi` is not in conflict (both values ≥ 27) and, because the two values differ by more than the ±0.2 tolerance, is decided as usual with flag `inconsistent_values` (contract §3.1 step 5, §4). `Reviewed: yes`
- **S-05** `review`: evidence exists and a human must judge it: positive exclusion evidence, an uncertain diagnosis, a policy grey zone, an imprecise source, a table, a combination pill, an unrecognized wording. Example: "Sleep study years ago showed OSA; has not used CPAP since tonsillectomy." → `exclusion.osa` `review` (severity unspecified, post-surgical status unknown). `Reviewed: yes`
- **S-06** Never invent a sixth status, and never encode "needs a look" as `insufficient` to lower the review count: the human-workload metric counts `review` + `conflict` + `insufficient` together, so the choice must follow the definitions above, not the count. `Reviewed: yes`
- **S-07** The condition `reason` field is a short English sentence that names the deciding evidence in the single public form `doc_id[start:end] sha8` (contract §11, U-05); it never pastes the quote. Example: "BMI at initiation below 35; see D2-clinic-note[812:833] 5d1e0b9a". `Reviewed: yes`

## 3. Assertion: three axes

Every fact carries `certainty`, `subject` and `temporality` (contract §6). The axes are labelled independently and may combine freely (for example uncertain + family + historical).

### 3.1 Definitions

- **A-01** `certainty`: `present` — the text asserts the fact; `negated` — the text denies it; `uncertain` — the text proposes, suspects, questions, or leaves it pending. `Reviewed: yes`
- **A-02** `subject`: `patient` — the person the document is about; `family` — a relative; `other` — anyone else (a spouse, a donor, a population statement). `Reviewed: yes`
- **A-03** `temporality`: `current` — holds at the time the sentence describes; `historical` — happened in the past and the text does not say it has ended ("history of", "prior", "s/p"); `resolved` — the text itself says it has ended, remitted or been cured; `hypothetical` — mentioned as a risk, a possibility, a condition, or a counselling point, not as something the patient has. A time expression after the mention ("…, diagnosed two years ago") is not a cue and does not make the fact `historical`; see C-08. `Reviewed: yes`
- **A-04** Treated is not resolved. "s/p", "post", "after surgery", "on CPAP", "controlled on medication" describe treatment and keep `temporality` at `current` or `historical`; only an explicit statement of remission, resolution or cure earns `resolved`. `Reviewed: yes`
- **A-05** Default values are present / patient / current. A mention outside the scope of every cue takes the defaults. The annotator does not infer a non-default value from world knowledge; a non-default value needs a cue in the sentence. `Reviewed: yes`

- **A-06** A cue acts only inside its scope: to its right, until the next scope terminator. Terminators: `but`, `however`, `except`, `;`, the end of the sentence, and a list-item boundary (a line break that starts a new bullet, number or table row). Candidate terminators not yet adopted: `although`, `whereas`, `while`, `:`. In a statement that no cause was found for a diagnosis, the negation applies to the cause. Do not add or negate a diagnosis fact solely from that statement. Label an independent assertion of the diagnosis under the usual rules (P-06). `Reviewed: yes`
- **A-07** A cue to the left of the mention within the same scope applies; a cue to the right ("… was ruled out") applies only when the pattern is in the table as a right-hand pattern (`ruled out`, `excluded`, `resolved`, `in remission`, `pending`). Contract §6 describes cue scope as acting to the right of the cue only; these right-hand patterns are a guide extension. Gold labels what the text says; until `check_assertion` implements them, such a sentence makes the system return `unrecognized` → `review`, which is scored as a miss and is not corrected in gold (P-05; contract follow-up: §6 right-hand pattern list). When the annotator cannot tell whether a cue reaches the mention, the fact is labelled with the defaults and the condition is `review` with reason `unrecognized`. `Reviewed: yes`
- **A-08** `?` counts as `uncertain` only when attached to a concept ("?HFpEF", "HFpEF?"); a question mark ending a question sentence is not a cue. A `?` attached on the right is a right-hand pattern in the sense of A-07. `Reviewed: yes`
- **A-09** Negation of a treatment is not negation of the condition. "Not on insulin" leaves diabetes unlabelled; "no CPAP" leaves OSA unlabelled. `Reviewed: yes`
- **A-10** Family cue scope: "Family history: mother with diabetes, father with stroke; patient denies diabetes or stroke" → in the first scope, diabetes and stroke take subject `family`; the `;` terminates that scope, and `denies` opens a new one that covers both following mentions → two more facts, `negated` / `patient` / `current`. Four facts in total; none is dropped. `Reviewed: yes`

Use `review` with flag `unrecognized` when the ambiguity prevents a condition decision. This is an annotation exception to A-05. Verifier cue scope remains sentence-local under contract §6. `Reviewed: no`

## 4. Timepoints

- **T-01** `relative_to_glp1_initiation` ∈ `at` / `before` / `after` / `unknown` is labelled on every fact. The annotator labels what the text supports; the program recomputes it from dates (`relative_computed`) and scoring uses the program's value, so a gold date must be right before a gold timepoint matters (contract §5). `Reviewed: yes`
- **T-02** `at`: decided in two steps. A fact with an explicit date is compared by date interval: `at` only when the interval is the single day of the initiation date (contract §5 item 1); baseline wording is not consulted. A fact with no date is `at` only when the quote itself names it as the initiation baseline ("at initiation", "baseline", "before starting <drug>" in the sense of the pre-treatment measurement; item 2). Any other undated fact is `unknown`, not `at`. Only `at` weight and stated BMI feed the BMI sub-results, height may come from any date (C-02); `before` alone gives `review` (contract §3.1 step 2; project policy `initiation_bmi_accepts: [at]`). Example: "Weight 102 kg today" in a note dated two weeks before the start date → `before`; the same sentence in a note dated the start day → `at`. `Reviewed: yes`
- **T-03** `before` / `after`: the fact's whole date interval lies before / after the initiation date. A month-precision date in the same month as initiation overlaps and is `unknown`. Example: initiation 2025-03-15; a weight dated 2025-02-28 → `before`; dated 2025-03 → `unknown`; dated 2025-04-02 → `after`. `Reviewed: yes`
- **T-04** `event_date` is written at the precision the text gives: `2024-01-15` (`day`), `2024-01` (`month`), `2024` (`year`). Never complete a month to its first day. A date the text does not contain is `null`; the timepoint is then `unknown` unless the quote itself carries the explicit baseline wording of T-02, in which case it is `at` with `event_date: null`. `Reviewed: yes`
- **T-05** `doc_dates[]`: an undated main document (MTSamples) gets its date only from a sentence in another visible document that states it ("the attached consult note from 2025-03-14"). That sentence is a gold fact of field `doc_dates` with `target_doc_id` and `date`; it must itself pass quote verification and must not be excluded (wrong patient, PA draft; contract §3 step 0, §5 item 3), and it supplies a date only to undated facts in the target document that describe that visit (the vitals, the examination, the current medication list); a fact whose quote carries its own time reference keeps it: an explicit date is kept as written, and a relative expression ("weight was 130 kg a year ago") leaves `event_date` null and the timepoint `unknown` rather than taking the document date (how the program treats such a fact is a contract follow-up: §5 item 3). Without such a usable sentence, only the undated facts of the target document stay `unknown` (facts with their own date keep it), except facts whose own quote carries the explicit baseline wording of T-02 (those are `at` without a date). The `doc_dates` fact itself carries `event_date` = the date it states for the target document (so its timepoint is that of the target document), not the date of the letter that contains it. Example: the referral letter says "I enclose Dr. A's consult note of 2025-03-14" → one `doc_dates` fact in `D1-referral` with `target_doc_id: D2-clinic-note`, `date: 2025-03-14`, default three axes; the undated vitals in D2 then carry `event_date: 2025-03-14` and, with initiation 2025-03-14, timepoint `at`; "MI in 2019" in the same note keeps `event_date: 2019`, and "weight was 130 kg a year ago" stays `unknown`. `Reviewed: yes`
- **T-06** A BMI `at` label for a BMI fact that carries no date of its own in an undated document therefore needs either explicit baseline wording inside the BMI quote (T-02), or two gold facts: the anthropometric fact and the `doc_dates` fact that dates the document to the initiation day. A BMI quote that names its own measurement date is compared by that date (T-02) and needs neither. Example: "BMI 36.5 measured 2025-03-15" in an undated note with initiation 2025-03-15 → `at` without a `doc_dates` fact. `Reviewed: yes`
- **T-07** `assessment_date` (in `input.yaml`) is the reference date for "current and ongoing" (lifestyle) and for nothing else; it is never the run date and never the initiation date. Example: `assessment_date` 2026-10-15; a nutrition program recorded as ended in 2026-06 is not current, whatever the run date. `Reviewed: yes`
- **T-08** The initiation date itself is a gold fact (`glp1_start`) and must be found in the visible text; `target_course` in `input.yaml` only says which course is assessed. If the text does not contain it, every BMI sub-result is `insufficient` regardless of what the annotator knows. Label evidence of the assessed course's initiation as `glp1_start` without duplicating the same initiation as `medications`. Apply P-06 to repeated evidence. In gold, record `purpose` from the stated reason (`weight`, `mace`, `other`) and `payer` from the text (G-12). Use `unknown` for each unstated field. Example: the referral letter says "started Wegovy on 2025-03-15" → `glp1_start` fact with `drug: Wegovy`, `date: 2025-03-15`; if no document names the date, `bmi_path_a`, `bmi_path_b.bmi` and `bmi_path_c.bmi` are `insufficient` even though `target_course` carries it. `Reviewed: yes`
- **T-09** Medication periods: `start`, `end` and `end_kind` are labelled at the text's precision. An undated list labelled as current medications in the same document as a BP reading is in use on that document's date (`same_note_list`). Whether the list is explicitly complete (`medication_lists[].complete`) is labelled from wording such as "complete medication list" or "no other medications"; a section header alone is not completeness. Example: "Current medications: amlodipine 5 mg, metoprolol 50 mg" in a note dated 2025-03-14 that also records BP 152/94 → both drugs in use on 2025-03-14 (`same_note_list`); `complete: false` because the header does not say the list is complete. `Reviewed: yes`
- **T-11** Label a `medications` fact only for a drug recognized by the active drug table (currently `rules/drugs-v1.yaml`). Record an out-of-table medication candidate with `decision: reject` and note that it is outside the table. Record proposed additions for a separate versioned rules change. Apply T-08 and C-16 separately to `glp1_start` and `prior_glp1`. `Reviewed: no`
- **T-10** Exclusion evidence after initiation is still labelled and still counts (`post_initiation_exclusion` → `review`); the "after initiation is not used" rule applies to BMI and pathway facts only. Example: "A1c 7.1%, new diagnosis of type 2 diabetes" dated four months after initiation → `t2dm` fact, `after` → `exclusion.t2dm` `review` + `post_initiation_exclusion`. `Reviewed: yes`

## 5. Per-condition labelling rules

Each subsection points to the decision table in the contract and lists the traps that most often produce a wrong label (drawn from the review checklist). Facts are labelled first; the condition status follows from the facts and the contract table.

### 5.1 `age_18` (contract §3.5)

- **C-01** Label `dob` and `stated_age` from `patient_identity[]` with the document date; a stated age is an interval `[a, a+1)` on that day. Compare the stated age and the DOB-derived age at the same timepoint. Apply the age-18 threshold before the one-year tolerance (contract §4). When both ages fall on the same side of 18 and differ by more than one year, add `inconsistent_values`. Cite both ages and their evidence locations in the reason. Trap: taking an age stated years after initiation as the age at initiation without the interval shift. Example: "a 79-year-old woman" in a note dated 2023-05-10 with initiation on 2021-05-10 → age interval [77, 78) at initiation → `age_18` `supported`. `Reviewed: yes`

### 5.2 `bmi_path_a` and `*.bmi` (contract §3.1)

- **C-02** Label every height, weight and stated BMI as an `anthropometrics` fact with `raw_value`, `raw_unit` and `norm_value` (`cm`, `kg`, `kg/m2`). Units are normalized in `norm_value` only; `raw_*` keep the text. Thresholds are compared with the unrounded value (project policy `bmi_comparison: unrounded`) and only the `at` weight and stated BMI feed the sub-result (project policy `initiation_bmi_accepts: [at]`, T-02); height may come from the nearest record of any timepoint, with flag `height_from_other_date` (contract §3.1 step 1). The computed value and every stated value are all candidates against the threshold (step 3). With no `at` weight and no `at` stated BMI the contract order applies first: a `before` weight or BMI exists → `review`; otherwise `insufficient` (step 2). An `at` weight with no height anywhere and no stated BMI yields no candidate value → `insufficient`. A sub-result that would be `supported` becomes `review` when the evidence it uses carries `table_source`, `timing_disagreement` or `unrecognized` (contract §3.1 step 8); the annotator writes the flag and the `review`. Record converted height and weight `norm_value` to one decimal place in cm and kg. Calculate BMI and compare it with thresholds using unrounded values. Obesity descriptors alone produce no `anthropometrics` or `diagnoses` fact. Use `kind: weight` only for actual body-weight values, excluding weight changes, targets, and rates of loss. Example: the only `at` BMI sits in a `|`-delimited vitals table → `bmi_path_a` `review` + `table_source`. Example: "Height 5'8\", weight 240 lb" → height `raw_value: 5'8"`, `norm_value: 172.7` cm; weight `raw_value: 240`, `raw_unit: lb`, `norm_value: 108.9` kg; computed BMI 36.5. Traps: lb read as kg, in as cm; a current BMI used as the initiation BMI; a measurement before bariatric surgery used as the initiation value; any pre-initiation measurement treated as `at`. `Reviewed: yes`
- **C-03** A pediatric expression (a percentage of a percentile, a z-score) is `kind: bmi_percentile` and never a kg/m² value. `Reviewed: no`
- **C-04** Two BMI values in one document are two facts; if they straddle a threshold the sub-result using that threshold is `conflict`; on the same side and more than ±0.2 apart the status is decided as usual with `inconsistent_values`; within ±0.2 they count as one value (contract §4). Never keep only one of them. Example: "BMI 34.6" in the vitals and "BMI 36" in the assessment of the same note, both `at` → `bmi_path_a` `conflict`; `bmi_path_b.bmi` and `bmi_path_c.bmi` `supported` + `inconsistent_values`. `Reviewed: yes`
- **C-05** An integer stated BMI with no height and weight whose rounding could cross the threshold (contract §3.1 step 6) gives `review` + `imprecise_source` (project policy `bmi_precision_unknown: review`); two candidates both clearly above the threshold give `supported`, not `insufficient`. Example: "BMI 30" alone → `bmi_path_b.bmi` `review` + `imprecise_source` (the true value may be 29.6); "BMI 41" with height 172.7 cm and weight 122 kg (computed 40.9) → `bmi_path_a` `supported`. `Reviewed: yes`
- **C-06** Without a verified initiation date no BMI sub-result and no pathway is `supported`; this is an invariant, not a judgement. Example: a consult note with height, weight and BMI but no document naming the start date → `bmi_path_a`, `bmi_path_b.bmi`, `bmi_path_c.bmi` all `insufficient`. `Reviewed: yes`

### 5.3 `bmi_path_b.htn` (contract §3.2)

- **C-07** Label each BP reading (`sbp`, `dbp`) with its date; label each medication with `name`, `dose`, `indication` if stated, `start` / `end` / `end_kind`, `list_id`; label each list's `complete`. The status follows contract §3.2 top to bottom, readings first and drugs second: no `at` reading → `insufficient`; no reading with `sbp > 140` or `dbp > 90` → `insufficient`, and a reading of exactly 140 or 90 adds flag `boundary_value` (project policy `bp_threshold_strict: true`); an above-threshold reading with two or more antihypertensives in use on that day that each count unambiguously (deduplicated by `drug_canonical`, from different products, none of them `class_ambiguous`) → `supported`; two reached only through one combination pill → `review` + `combination_pill` (project policy `combination_pill_counts_as_two: review`); two reached only by counting a drug the drug table marks `class_ambiguous` (a diuretic in a heart-failure patient, `rules/drugs-v1.yaml`) → `review` + `class_ambiguous`, even when the reading is above threshold; fewer than two drugs and a list with `complete: true` → `not_supported` (project policy `htn_not_supported_by_complete_list: true`); anything else, including a medication period that cannot be shown to overlap the reading → `insufficient` (project policy `unknown_med_period: not_ongoing`). Readings alone never give `not_supported` (project policy `htn_not_supported_by_readings: false`). The annotator labels the drugs as medications; the drug table, not the annotator, decides `class_ambiguous`; a drug outside the drug table is not a `medications` fact (T-11). Example: "BP 140/88; on a diuretic combination pill and a loop diuretic for heart failure" → one `bp_readings` fact, two medication facts → `bmi_path_b.htn` `insufficient` + `boundary_value`, not `not_supported`. Second example: "BP 152/94; on amlodipine and furosemide for heart failure" → above threshold, but the second drug counts only through a `class_ambiguous` entry → `review` + `class_ambiguous`, not `supported`. Traps: exactly 140 or 90 read as "greater than"; a "controlled" statement overriding an above-threshold reading; one antihypertensive seen and the list assumed to be the whole list; a drug with another plausible indication (an alpha-blocker for urinary symptoms, a diuretic for heart failure) counted as an antihypertensive without the `class_ambiguous` route; a combination pill counted as two drugs without `combination_pill`; a narrative mention and a list entry of the same drug in the same document describing the same regimen are one fact (P-06); keep the one that carries the indication; a drug that appears only in the narrative or only in the list is still one fact; a diagnosis word alone ("HTN") taken as uncontrolled hypertension; pulmonary hypertension (present or negated) labelled as `hypertension`. `Reviewed: yes`

### 5.4 `bmi_path_b.hfpef` (contract §3.2)

- **C-08** Only an explicit diagnosis concept `hfpef` is labelled as such; "diastolic dysfunction", "preserved EF", "EF 60%" are findings and are not `hfpef` facts (G-07); "diastolic heart failure" and "heart failure with preserved EF" are `hfpef`. Generic heart failure wording such as heart failure, CHF, or decompensated HF produces neither an `hfpef` nor an `other` diagnosis fact. It contributes no evidence to `bmi_path_b.hfpef`. Label explicit HFpEF diagnoses and recognized diagnostic equivalents, including diastolic heart failure, under C-08. Status (contract §3.2): present / historical → `supported`; uncertain → `review`; negated → `not_supported`; a finding only → `insufficient`. Example: "History of HFpEF, first diagnosed two years ago" → one `hfpef` fact, present / patient / historical (cue `history of` precedes the mention) → `supported`; "Echo: grade II diastolic dysfunction, EF 62%" → no `hfpef` fact → `insufficient`. A time expression after the mention ("HFpEF, diagnosed two years ago", "hypertension since 2019") is onset information: no cue precedes the mention, so `temporality` keeps the default `current` (contract §6); a real date in it is written to `event_date` at the text's precision and a relative expression leaves `event_date` null (T-04). Traps: r/o HFpEF labelled present; a relative's HFpEF labelled subject `patient`. `Reviewed: yes`

### 5.5 `bmi_path_b.ckd` (contract §3.2)

### 5.6 `bmi_path_c.prediabetes` (contract §3.2)

### 5.7 `bmi_path_c.mi`, `.stroke` (contract §3.2)

### 5.14 `lifestyle.nutrition`, `.activity` (contract §3.5)

- **C-18** Label `program_type`, `attendance`, `start` / `end` / `end_kind`, `limitation`. A pending referral or scheduled first visit without documented attendance is `referral_pending`, including a routine pre-operative appointment. Determine applicability separately under the date and period rules above. Example: "Referred to the dietitian, first visit next month; currently following Noom" → one `referral_pending` fact and one `commercial` fact → `lifestyle.nutrition` `review`. Traps: advice or a pending referral labelled as a program; a declined single dietitian visit labelled as "no diet plan at all"; a commercial program labelled `structured_clinical`; a program that starts after `assessment_date` counted as current. `Reviewed: yes`

Pathway A equals the BMI ≥ 35 sub-result. Sub-results keep their own status whatever the pathway. Example: `bmi_path_b.bmi` `insufficient` (no height), `hfpef` `supported`, `htn` `insufficient`, `ckd` `review` → `bmi_path_b` `insufficient`. `Reviewed: yes`
- **C-20** `mace_purpose` is a case flag, not a status: label it in `case_flags` only when a verified, non-excluded `glp1_start` fact for the primary patient that refers to the course named in `target_course` has `purpose: mace` (the text states that this course is for cardiovascular risk reduction). ## 6. PA draft claims

- **K-01** The PA draft (`role: pa_draft`) is labelled only as `pa_form_claims[]` (`claim_id`, `claimed_value`). No clinical fact is ever labelled from it; its MBI and date of birth are labelled as `patient_identity[]` like any document's, because the identity table (contract §3.5) covers every document; a checked box is a claim, not evidence (`exclusion_reasons: claim_document`; project policy `pa_draft_claims_support_alone: false`). Example: the draft ticks "heart failure with preserved ejection fraction: Yes" → one `pa_form_claims` fact (`claim.bmi_path_b.hfpef`, `yes`); no `hfpef` diagnosis fact is written from it. `Reviewed: yes`
- **K-02** `claim_id` follows the condition it is checked against: `claim.age_18`, `claim.drug`, `claim.bmi_bracket`, `claim.bmi_path_b.hfpef`, `claim.exclusion.t2dm`, and so on (contract §2). `claimed_value` is the box or bracket as written (`yes`, `no`, `30-34.9`, a drug name). `pa_form_claims` facts always carry the default three axes; words in the form's item label (previous, history of) are the question's wording, not cues. `Reviewed: yes`
- **K-03** Claim status in gold `claims[]`: the chart supports the claimed value → `supported`; the chart says nothing → `insufficient`; the chart contradicts it → `conflict`; the chart mentions it only as suspected or with unclear status → `review`. A claim never changes the condition's own status. For a yes/no claim, compare the checked value with what the chart establishes about the question. Agreement is `supported` and contradiction is `conflict`. Use `review` when relevant evidence leaves the answer unresolved, such as a boundary reading, a suspected diagnosis, missing required detail, or conflicting evidence. Use `insufficient` when the chart lacks evidence that could answer the question, including advice or a pending referral alone for a claim of an ongoing program. Use a condition status directly only when it answers the claim. An explicit T2DM diagnosis contradicts a No claim even when the exclusion condition is `review`. Compare `claim.drug` against the documented drug for the assessed course and `claim.bmi_bracket` under K-04. Example: the draft ticks "type 2 diabetes: No"; the chart says "denies type 2 diabetes" → `claim.exclusion.t2dm` `supported`; the chart says "T2DM on metformin" → `conflict`. `Reviewed: yes`
- **K-04** `claim.bmi_bracket` is compared against the bracket chosen from the unrounded initiation BMI (`[27,30)`, `[30,35)`, `[35,40)`, `≥40`); a stated BMI of 29.95 is in `[27,30)` (project policy `bmi_bucket_from_raw: true`). The form's "Less than 27" box is `claimed_value: <27` and is compared against `[0,27)` the same way (contract follow-up: §10 lists brackets from `[27,30)` only). Example: unrounded initiation BMI 29.95 and a draft ticking "30 to 34.9" → `claim.bmi_bracket` `conflict`. `Reviewed: yes`

Example: "CKD; eGFR 52 on 2025-03-14" → one `ckd` fact with `ckd_stage: null`, one `labs` fact → `review`, reason naming eGFR 52 (2025-03-14).

- Source: CMS: [CMS PA form](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/glp-1-bridge.pdf) Q12 "Does the patient have chronic kidney disease stage 3a or above?" (undated 3-page form, retrieved 2026-11-08; PDF metadata: file modified 2026-06-03, filename marks a 2026-06-01 draft updated before 2026-07-01; the Bridge landing page that links it was last modified 2026-07-13). Project policy: reading rather than computing the stage (`ckd_dx_egfr_only: review`; contract §3.2). Checked by the coding agent 2026-11-08; Terri confirms.

### G-10 OSA severity is read, not inferred

`osa_severity` is `moderate` / `severe` when the text writes it or when an AHI ≥ 15 is written; `mild` when written or AHI < 15; otherwise `unspecified`. CPAP use, a sleep study without a result, or a referral does not set severity.

- Source: CMS: [CMS PA form](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/glp-1-bridge.pdf) Q2 "Does the patient have moderate to severe obstructive sleep apnea (OSA)?" (undated 3-page form, retrieved 2026-11-08; PDF metadata: file modified 2026-06-03, filename marks a 2026-06-01 draft updated before 2026-07-01; the Bridge landing page that links it was last modified 2026-07-13). ### G-11 Treated, controlled or operated is not resolved

CMS asks the prescriber to distinguish "no longer applies" from "well controlled". The annotator makes no such distinction: a treated or controlled exclusion diagnosis is labelled `present` and the exclusion is `review`; only a textual statement of remission or resolution is `resolved`, and the exclusion is still `review`.

- Source: CMS: [CMS Information for Providers FAQ](https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coverage/prescription-drug-coverage/medicare-glp-1-bridge/information-providers) (page last modified 2026-08-06): prescribers distinguish "cases in which a diagnosis may no longer apply and those in which a condition is well controlled by medication or other interventions". Clinical: none. Project policy: the always-`review` handling (contract §3.3; no rules key). Checked by the coding agent 2026-11-08; Terri confirms.
- Reviewed: no

## 10. Adjudication log

One line per adjudicated item in `cases/<variant_id>/adjudication.jsonl` (n2c2-style). Lines are appended, never rewritten.

- **L-01** Fields (draft): `variant_id`; `item` (`fact` or `condition`); `field` and `kind` / `concept`, or `condition_id`; `candidate` — the proposing model's candidate as an object restricted to the allow-list below, or `null` when none; `scan_hit` — the uncovered keyword hit as (`doc_id`, `start`, `end`, `sha8`, `term`) or `null`; `inventory` — the blind-draft presence for this field or `null`; `decision` ∈ `accept` / `modify` / `reject` / `add` / `absent`; `final` — the gold entry restricted to the same allow-list, or `null`. Allow-list for `candidate` and `final`, in two groups: position keys `doc_id`, `start`, `end`, `sha8` (quote offsets only); value keys `field`, `kind`, `concept`, `norm_value`, `norm_unit`, `sbp`, `dbp`, `event_date`, `date_precision`, `period_start`, `period_end` (medication or program periods, never under `start` / `end`), `end_kind`, `certainty`, `subject`, `temporality`, `relative_to_glp1_initiation`, `drug_canonical`, `program_type`, `attendance`, `osa_severity`, `ahi`, `fibrosis_stage`, `cirrhosis`, `ckd_stage`, `symptomatic`, `payer`, `purpose`, `claim_id`, `claimed_value`, `target_doc_id`, `date` (for `doc_dates`), `status` (for `item: condition`, blind in `inventory` and final in `final`), `identity_match` ∈ `match` / `mismatch` / `missing` (for `patient_identity`, instead of the identifier itself). Every other key, in particular `quote`, `raw_value`, `raw_unit`, `description`, `indication`, `section_title`, `name`, `patient_id` and `dob`, is never written; text and identifiers are referenced by position only (P-07); `guideline_clause` — the clause ID relied on (any clause prefix of this guide) or `null`; `guideline_version`; `drafted_by` — snapshot-level model ID of the candidate-proposing model, or `null` (for authored documents the drafting model is recorded in `case.yaml` `documents[].drafted_by`, P-02); `decided_at`; `note` — free text that paraphrases; no run of four or more consecutive words copied from a document. Example: `{"variant_id": "case-01", "item": "fact", "field": "diagnoses", "concept": "t2dm", "candidate": {"doc_id": "D2-clinic-note", "start": 1540, "end": 1562, "sha8": "9b3c1e7a", "certainty": "present", "subject": "patient", "temporality": "current"}, "scan_hit": null, "inventory": "present", "decision": "modify", "final": {"doc_id": "D2-clinic-note", "start": 1540, "end": 1562, "sha8": "9b3c1e7a", "field": "diagnoses", "concept": "t2dm", "certainty": "present", "subject": "family", "temporality": "current", "event_date": null, "relative_to_glp1_initiation": "unknown"}, "guideline_clause": "A-10", "guideline_version": 1, "drafted_by": "proposing-model-snapshot-2026-11-20", "decided_at": "2026-11-21", "note": "family-history sentence naming type 2; cue mother in scope"}`. `Reviewed: yes`
- **L-02** Every scored gold fact has at least one adjudication line, whatever its `origin` (`spec`, `model_candidate`, `keyword_scan`, `human_inventory`); every `expected_absent` entry has a line with `decision: absent` and the inventory and scan results it rests on. Example: `{"item": "fact", "field": "diagnoses", "concept": "mash", "candidate": null, "scan_hit": null, "inventory": "absent", "decision": "absent", "final": null}`. `Reviewed: yes`
- **L-03** No second-opinion model is used (05-ai-workflow §5.2, decided 2026-11-08): items the annotator cannot settle are decided by the annotator and the line records the decision and its clause. If that decision is ever reversed, a counter-view is reference only, and the line records that it was asked and what was decided, never its text. Example: a candidate the annotator cannot place in or out of a negation scope → `decision: modify` to the default axes, `guideline_clause: A-07`, condition `review` with reason `unrecognized`. `Reviewed: yes`

## 11. Writing uncertainty reasons

The `reason` field of a `review`, `conflict` or `insufficient` condition tells the reviewer what to look at.

- **U-01** One sentence, English, in the form "<what is missing or unclear>; see <doc_id>[start:end] sha8". Name the policy or clause when one applies (`G-xx`, `boundary_value`, `imprecise_source`). Example: "no weight or BMI dated at initiation, only a weight from 2024-11 (before); see D2-clinic-note[1203:1241] 3f9a2c7e". `Reviewed: yes`
- **U-02** For `conflict`, name both values with their `doc_id` locations and the threshold they straddle. Example: "BMI 29.6 (D2-clinic-note[812:833] 5d1e0b9a) vs 30.4 (D1-referral[402:433] a71c04f2) straddle 30". `Reviewed: yes`
- **U-03** For `review` on an exclusion, state the three-axis label and what a human has to decide (severity, stage, current status, payer year), in the annotator's own words, never a phrase lifted from the document. Example: "t2dm present / patient / resolved (remission after bariatric surgery); current status to be judged; see D1-referral[220:268] c08d9e14". `Reviewed: yes`
- **U-04** For `insufficient`, say which kind of evidence would have been needed (a dated measurement at initiation, a diagnosis statement, a payer). Do not write "not mentioned" alone when something related was mentioned. Example: "no height anywhere; weight 96 kg at admission cannot give a BMI; a height or a stated BMI dated at initiation would be needed; see D2-published-note[640:662] 7e2b5a90". `Reviewed: yes`
- **U-05** Never paste document text into `reason`; never name the patient; never include MTSamples offsets in public reports except in the `doc_id[start:end] sha8` form. `Reviewed: yes`

## 12. Reliability checks and guide revision

- **R-01** Revision cadence: after the 2 smoke-test cases are labelled, the guide is revised once before the remaining cases; later revisions are batched. Every revision bumps the guideline version and lists (a) the clauses changed and (b) the frozen gold items that could be affected; only those items are re-examined, and any change to them is written to that gold's `changes` with the clause ID. `Reviewed: no`
- **R-02** Self-retest: two real-text dev cases are relabelled about a week after the first pass, with the earlier labels and the candidates hidden. The report gives, per case, agreeing conditions / total conditions, plus the differences in three-axis labels, timepoints and missed evidence. Agreement is not correctness and is reported as agreement. `Reviewed: no`
- **R-03** Independent blind set: 3–5 cases are chosen and labelled by a colleague following this guide, unseen by Terri until evaluation; for 1–2 of them both annotators label conditions and facts independently before seeing any output, both sets are saved before adjudication, and condition-level agreement is reported separately. This checks whether the guide is clear; it is not a full inter-annotator agreement study and is not described as one. `Reviewed: no`
- **R-04** Optional seeded errors: 5–8 known-wrong candidates (fixed seed) are mixed into the verification queue and revealed afterwards; the report states how many were caught. `Reviewed: no`
- **R-05** Disclosure: the report lists the share of gold by `origin`, the number of facts found only by human inventory, and the number decided by `G-xx` clauses. `Reviewed: no`
