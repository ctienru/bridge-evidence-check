# Annotation guide — revision 03

Status: Applied to guide version 3 on 2026-11-23

Scope: the holdout gold round (cases 11, 14 and 16) left 20 guide clauses that needed interpretation. Each now has a decision. The locations and the case detail behind each item are kept in the private repository, so this file gives the clause, the change and the decision only. No document text, value or location from a holdout case appears here; the examples are invented.

How to read this file. One row per item: the clause, the decided wording as it was placed in [`annotation-guide.md`](./annotation-guide.md), and the decision. Row IDs are `R03-nn` to keep them apart from guide clauses.

| R03-08 | K-03, K-01 | K-03, add: A claim is decided on its own question. The clinical sub-result keeps its own status, and the two may differ. When the chart holds material related to the claim that cannot answer it, the claim is `review`, also when the sub-result is `insufficient`. Related material is a written diagnosis that the claim builds on while the claim's further requirements are not shown, or a lab inside the range that the claimed diagnosis is defined by, with no diagnosis statement. The claim is `insufficient` only when the chart holds nothing related. A lab outside that range with no diagnosis statement is not related material, and a source or format flag on the sub-result (`table_source`, `unrecognized`, `imprecise_source`) does not by that alone make the claim `review`. Example (invented): the draft ticks uncontrolled hypertension on two or more drugs; the chart names hypertension and gives no reading at initiation → `bmi_path_b.htn` `insufficient`, `claim.bmi_path_b.htn` `review`. | accepted |
| R03-09 | T-05, T-02, T-03 | T-05, add after the relative-expression sentence: This holds even when the expression points clearly earlier than the dated visit. The annotator does not compute a date or an order from a relative expression. | accepted |
| R03-10 | T-02, T-04, T-05, T-06, P-06 | T-04, add: When the quote carries the baseline wording of T-02 and the document is dated by a verified `doc_dates` fact, the fact is `at` and takes that date as `event_date`; the wording decides the timepoint and the document date supplies the calendar day. If the two disagree (baseline wording in a document dated to another day), keep `at`, set `event_date: null`, and record the disagreement in the adjudication `note`. P-06, add: A value written in prose and restated in a table of the same document is one fact. Place the span on the prose sentence. `table_source` applies only when the table row is the only place the value is written. | accepted |
| R03-11 | T-08, C-16, P-06 | T-08, add: One initiation is one `glp1_start` fact for the whole case, not one per document (an exception to P-06). The fact lives on the sentence that states the start date. A sentence in another document that refers to the same start without adding a date is not a second fact; record it with `decision: reject` and a note that names the fact it repeats. C-16, add: The same holds for one prior course. The fact lives on the sentence that states the payer. A detail that only a repeated reference states is recorded in the adjudication `note` and is not written into the fact. | accepted |
| R03-12 | C-18, T-07 | C-18, replace the applicability and first status sentences with: A record counts only if it is shown to apply on `assessment_date`: its period covers that day, or it is dated on that day, or it is dated before that day with `end_kind: ongoing`. A record dated before that day that says nothing about continuing is `end_kind: unknown` and does not count. A programme that starts later never counts. `structured_clinical` and shown to apply on `assessment_date` → `supported`. Add: `attendance: regular` needs recorded attendance, such as return visits that took place at a stated interval; a planned schedule alone is `unknown`. `end_kind: ongoing` needs wording that the programme continues at the time the document describes. | accepted |
| R03-13 | T-05, A-03, P-09 | T-05, add: The verified document date is the date on which a history or family-history statement was recorded. A diagnosis or family-history fact takes it as `event_date`; the fact keeps its own `temporality`, and the date is never read as an onset date. Precedence: a date written in the mention itself wins; a relative-time cue in the mention leaves `event_date` null and the timepoint `unknown` (the relative-expression rule above); otherwise the document date applies. T-05, also add: When no visible sentence dates a document, or a sentence says the document is undated, no `doc_dates` fact is written for it and its facts follow the rule above for undated documents. A document that narrates a period longer than one visit is not dated by the annotator to any single day. The absence of a `doc_dates` fact is recorded as one adjudication line with `decision: absent` and is not written to `expected_absent`. | accepted |
| R03-14 | C-01 | C-01, add: A stated age enters the comparison only when its document has a date, from the quote or from a verified `doc_dates` fact. A stated age in an undated document has no timepoint: it is still recorded as `stated_age`, it is not shifted to the initiation date, it is not paired with the DOB-derived age, and it adds neither `conflict` nor `inconsistent_values`. With a date of birth, `age_18` is decided from the date of birth alone. With no date of birth and only an undated stated age, `age_18` is `insufficient`. | accepted |
| R03-15 | C-16, T-08 | C-16, add: An earlier course is a prior course only when the text shows that it ended as a course of its own, by a stated end or a stated discontinuation. This holds whether the earlier course is written under the same product name, another brand, or the generic name of the same substance; `drug_canonical` may then be equal on the `prior_glp1` fact and the `glp1_start` fact. A pause followed by resumption, a dose change, or a brand switch without a stated end is the same course (contract §5) and gives no `prior_glp1` fact for an earlier course. A course of a different drug is a prior course as before. One exception to the rule that a `prior_glp1` fact is a course other than the assessed one: any statement that Part D paid for the assessed course is recorded as a `prior_glp1` fact with `payer: part_d`, so that `exclusion.part_d_glp1` can read it; `date` is filled when the text gives one and is null otherwise, and each dated fill is one fact. This holds for Part D only: another payer of the assessed course is still recorded only in the adjudication `note` (T-08), and the `glp1_start` fact still has no `payer` key. | accepted with one added sentence (included) |

## 4. Contract follow-ups

- Scoring of a denial of diabetes that names no type (R03-02): on hold, needs a design decision.
- `lifestyle` applicability in the rules (R03-12): closed in this change; a record dated before `assessment_date` with `end_kind: unknown` no longer applies (contract §3.5).
- A `program_type` value for self-directed records (R03-16): open.
- Relative time expressions (R03-09, R03-13): closed in this change for measurements and diagnoses; such a fact no longer inherits the document date (contract §5). Medication and lifestyle entries keep the document date, because the expression in them states when the period began.
- The claim rule for a sub-result that is `insufficient` while related material exists (R03-08): closed in this change (contract §2).
